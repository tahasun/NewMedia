California housing in 3D View code

Using data to tell a story. All 20,640 census block groups in California, binned into hexagons over a map with deck.gl. Colour encodes median home value; height reveals household income. Two dimensions of inequality made visible at a glance.

Convocation Hall: view from your seat View code

Convocation Hall is a round hall with two balconies of lettered sections, used for everything from convocations to concerts and conferences. While working in the university department that rents it out for internal and external events, I found the space was notoriously hard to describe to people who hadn't been inside.

So I built a gallery on top of a floor plan of the hall. Choose a section and you see a photo taken from there, looking toward the stage, so organizers and guests can picture the room before they arrive.
